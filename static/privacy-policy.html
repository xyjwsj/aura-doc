Aura Privacy Policy

A private journal, powered by your own AI key.

Last updated: 2026-10-20
Short version: Aura stores your journal on your device. We do not require an account, we do not operate servers that collect your data, and we do not run analytics on you. The only time your words leave your device is when you choose to use AI features — and then they are sent over the internet to the AI provider you configured yourself (bring your own key, or "BYOK"). Your API key stays on your device and we never see your text.

1. What Aura Is

Aura is a personal journaling app with optional AI assistance for reflection, mood tracking, daily summaries, and weekly and monthly insights. It is designed around a single principle: your private thoughts should stay private, and you stay in control of any AI you use.

2. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. Aura does not transmit, upload, or store your journal entries, mood logs, tags, or AI conversations on any server operated by us. We do not operate user accounts and we do not run analytics that identify you. Everything you write is kept locally on your device.

3. Device-Only Storage

All journaling — entries, moods, tags, and conversation history — is stored locally on your phone. No internet connection is required to write, review, or read your journal. We have no backend that receives or holds your data.

4. AI Features and Third-Party Providers (BYOK)

Aura's AI features (the companion chat, your daily summary, and reflections) call an online AI provider that you set up yourself. You paste your own API key and choose an endpoint — for example OpenAI, DeepSeek, Qwen, Moonshot, Tencent, CodeBuddy, a self-hosted model, or any OpenAI-compatible service.

  • When you use an AI feature, the relevant text (your entry, your message, or your day's digest) is sent over HTTPS directly to the provider you selected.
  • Your API key is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us.
  • We do not receive, store, or process that content on any server of ours. The data you send is governed by your chosen provider's own privacy policy, not ours.
  • Because you bring your own key and pay the provider directly, Aura's AI is unlimited on every plan and our marginal AI cost is zero.

5. No Third-Party Sharing by Us

Because we do not collect your data, we have nothing to sell, share, or disclose to advertisers, partners, or authorities. We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA) or third-party tracking SDKs. The only third party that may receive your text is the AI provider you explicitly configure — and only when you use an AI feature.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

  • Access & Portability: Your data lives in the app; you can export it anytime as Markdown, JSON, or (on Aura+) a beautiful PDF / print.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling Aura removes all locally stored data. There is no server copy for us to delete, because we never collect it.
  • No sale of data: We do not sell personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

Aura is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, simply delete the app to remove all associated local data.

8. Crisis Support

Aura is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. If you are in distress, the app can show local crisis helpline numbers (for example, 988 in the US). In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will update this page if our practices change. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact
